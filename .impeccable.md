# Design Context

## Users
A personal local-first workbench for a creator/researcher managing daily plans, fitness, AI learning, English practice, and research. It is used repeatedly through the day for quick task capture, focused execution, and short weekly reviews on desktop and mobile.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, and work-focused. The interface should feel like a research workspace rather than a gamified productivity app: structured, trustworthy, and quietly motivating.

## Aesthetic Direction
Use the research panel as the visual reference: light blue-gray background, white panels, fine blue-tinted borders, restrained blue as the primary accent, compact metadata, and dense but readable information hierarchy. Avoid decorative gradients, oversized metric cards, and generic equal-card grids.

## Design Principles
1. Surface the next actionable item and current progress before secondary detail.
2. Reuse the research workspace language across life-management pages without copying research-specific layouts.
3. Use page-scoped CSS for new modules so unrelated pages retain their existing presentation.
4. Keep all essential actions keyboard and touch accessible, with visible focus states and responsive single-column fallbacks.
5. Preserve existing local data contracts unless an explicit migration is implemented.
